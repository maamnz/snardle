# Snardle — Savage Wordle 😈

A classic, minimal Wordle clone featuring a savage AI narrator that roasts your gameplay on every guess.

## Features
- **Savage AI Narrator**: Context-aware roasts for terrible openers, all-gray attempts, choking on 4 greens, reusing eliminated gray letters, and fluke wins.
- **Minimal & Clean Design**: Authentic Wordle aesthetics, smooth flip animations, and zero clutter.
- **Dual Play Modes**:
  - **Daily Mode**: Synchronized daily word.
  - **Endless Mode**: Unlimited play with random words.
- **Stats & Share**: Streak tracking and clipboard emoji grid sharing.

## Play Online / Locally
Simply open `index.html` in your browser.
